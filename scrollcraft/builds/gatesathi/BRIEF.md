# ScrollCraft Build Brief: GateSathi

Status: Self-authored under explicit creative delegation from the user ("Make it bilingual in English and Hindi, and use the Scrollcraft skill to enhance the scrolling effects. Also use a few AI images to create a proper story-driven landing page. If he's scrolling, he gets the full story: how it works, what's important, and all the details.")

## 1. Eight Core Decisions

1. **Vibe (3 to 5 words + references)**: Warm civic documentary, trustworthy, grounded, human. References: Satyajit Ray morning school courtyard compositions, National Institute of Design (NID) bilingual civic typography, Kodak Portra 400 photojournalism.
2. **Scroll Journey (section by section)**:
   - Header: GateSathi brand mark, bilingual language switcher (`English` / `हिन्दी`), chapter folio indicator, and `Login` / `Open app` action.
   - Act I (Recognition, Dimensional Layered Hero): Morning at the school gate. Background courtyard plane, mid-ground guard-and-parent photographic plate, foreground floating bilingual Gate Verification Card plane, and headline `"Har entry, har bachcha, surakshit. / हर एंट्री, हर बच्चा, सुरक्षित।"`
   - Act II (Tension, The Paper Register Blind Spot): Hard cut to the worn-out paper register (`reveal` wipe + staggered problem cards): heavy paper register, no way to know who met which child, principal has no live view.
   - Act III (The Turn & Peak, How GateSathi Works Step-by-Step): Pinned interactive story stage (`pin`, span `3.2`) with our bespoke Signature Move (`data-gatesathi-pass`): as the visitor scrolls, a live bilingual Gate Entry Pass progresses through Step 1 (Guard enters 10-digit phone number), Step 2 (Live photo + student linked + approved guardian list checked, with unapproved pickup double-confirmation alert), and Step 3 (Principal sees live campus status in real time).
   - Act IV (Range, Complete 6-Capability Rail): Horizontal travel (`pan`, span `3.4`) across all 6 core capabilities with bilingual titles and operational detail.
   - Act V (Substance, Privacy, DPDP Act 2023 & Free for Government Schools): Editorial asymmetric spread (`flow` + `data-sc-in` + `data-sc-stagger`) pairing the Principal's sunlit office photograph with the 5 strict privacy guarantees and the unconditional `"Free for government schools"` commitment.
   - Act VI (Commitment, Request a Pilot): Calm, grounded pilot request form (`leads` collection) and footer (`GateSathi`, `"Built by Jayant Solution"`, `/privacy` link).
3. **Energy Curve**: Warm, dignified opening -> sober, high-contrast tension on the paper register -> expansive, kinetic clarity during the 3-step gate verification peak -> brisk lateral exploration across the 6 features -> calm institutional assurance on privacy & free access -> settled, confident close at the pilot form.
4. **Feeling Curve & Peak**:
   - Act I (Hero): **Warm recognition** caused by the layered morning school gate scene and bilingual promise of safety.
   - Act II (Problem): **Unease and urgency** caused by the reveal wipe of the smudged paper register and the three named safety blind spots.
   - Act III (How It Works - **PEAK**): **Relief and conviction** caused by watching a visitor entry assemble live on screen from phone number to guardian match to principal alert as the wheel turns.
   - Act IV (Features Rail): **Practical confidence** caused by lateral browsing of the six concrete capabilities built for basic Android phones.
   - Act V (Privacy & Free Mandate): **Institutional trust** caused by clear DPDP Act 2023 rules, zero Aadhaar/face-recognition, and the `"Free for government schools"` pledge.
   - Act VI (Pilot Request Close): **Readiness to act** caused by a simple 5-field pilot form that resolves and holds at the bottom of the story.
   - **The Peak Sentence**: "It's the site where scrolling through the school gate story actually fills out and verifies a live guardian gate pass right in front of you." (Lives in Act III).
5. **Signature Move**: **Scroll-Driven Bilingual Gate Pass Stamp (`data-gatesathi-pass`)**. Driven by Act III's `--sc-p` progress (`0..1`), an authentic bilingual GateSathi digital slip updates its state across three checkpoints: dialing the 10-digit phone (`98765 43210`), snapping the rear-camera photo + linking siblings (`Aarav & Ananya, Class 4A/2B`) + checking the approved guardian list (`Father: Verified`), and broadcasting the live campus badge (`Inside Now: 14:10 IST`) to the principal's desk.
6. **Aesthetic Range**: Documentary Editorial (warm alabaster `#F8FAFC`, deep school-uniform navy `#1E3A8A`, marigold saffron `#F59E0B`, deep slate ink `#0F172A`).
7. **World Structure**: Distinct chaptered scenes with hard-cut grounds and varied scroll devices.
8. **Assets**: GateSathi brand kit (`/brand/logo-horizontal.png`, `/brand/icon-white.png`) + 4 cohesive Kodak Portra 400 documentary photographs generated for each story chapter (`/story/01-school-gate-hero.png` through `/story/04-principal-live-peace.png`).
